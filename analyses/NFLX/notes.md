## Verdict

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-19 -->
**Cheap against its own history. At $60.2 (Nov 18 close) Netflix trades at 20.6x adjusted earnings, its lowest valuation in a decade outside the 2022 crash and below its 3-year low of 21.2x. Earnings still grow about 15% a year, but revenue growth has slowed four quarters running and advertising is behind plan. ## Today

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-19 -->
The stock closed at $60.2 on Nov 18, down about 3% since Nov 13, after touching a new 52-week low of about $59.40 on Nov 17. There was no new company news this week; the fall continues October's reaction to a cut in the 2026 advertising forecast (from about $3bn to $2.7bn) and to a lower Q4 margin.

**What it means for you:** the market now prices Netflix like a slow grower: about 20x earnings for a company whose earnings still grow about 15% a year and which generates about $12bn of free cash flow. That is the kind of mismatch Lynch looks for, but only if subscriber engagement holds. The next real test is the Q4 report on Jan 19. Until then, the price is moving with the market, not with new facts.

## Summary

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-19 -->
- **Cheap:** 20.6x adjusted earnings, the lowest in a decade outside 2022, against a 5-year median of about 41x; the price implies only about 8% a year cash-flow growth.
- **Strong:** large and growing free cash flow (about $12bn guided for 2026) and modest debt.
- **Weak:** guidance has disappointed three quarters running, and the advertising business is behind plan.
- **Uncertain:** how much of the slowdown is lasting, and what the new content chief and job cuts change.

## Business

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-19 -->
Netflix is the largest paid streaming service, earning almost all its revenue from subscriptions, with a growing advertising tier. Q3 2026 revenue was about $12.9bn, up about 12% a year, with EPS of $0.87 against a $0.82 estimate. The stock split 10-for-1 in November 2025. Its failed bid for Warner Bros. Discovery's studios and streaming business left it with a $2.8bn termination fee in Q1 2026, which the adjusted EPS here leaves out.

## Risks

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-19 -->
- **Slowing growth:** full-year 2026 revenue was trimmed to about $51.1bn, and Q4 EPS guidance of $0.67 is below the $0.73 consensus.
- **Advertising:** the 2026 ad revenue forecast was cut from about $3bn to $2.7bn because of delays in ad product features.
- **Costs:** about 1,000 job cuts (about 6%) will reduce the Q4 operating margin by about 1.2 points.
- **Competition** for viewing time from YouTube, short video and the combined Paramount–Warner Bros.
