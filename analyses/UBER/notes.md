## Summary

- **Cheap:** not quite; about 22x non-GAAP earnings, fair for EPS growth of 35-40% a year.
- **Strong:** operating income up about 30% a year, little capital spending (about $0.3bn a year) and modest net debt (about $10bn).
- **Weak:** reported EPS is dominated by tax releases and revaluations of its stakes, so the own-history P/E is not a reliable guide.
- **Uncertain:** whether robotaxis (Waymo, Tesla) bypass Uber's network or run on it.

## Business

Uber runs ride-hailing (Mobility), food and grocery delivery (Delivery) and Freight in about 70 countries. Q2 2026 revenue was about $14.2bn; GAAP income from operations rose about 30% to $1.9bn and non-GAAP EPS rose 35% to $0.81. Uber holds stakes in Didi, Grab, Aurora, Delivery Hero and others, whose revaluations swing GAAP EPS by about $1.5bn a quarter either way; Q3 2025 also included a $4.9bn tax benefit. The adjusted EPS here is Uber's non-GAAP EPS (see `eps_override` in the config): about $3.12 over the last 12 months (Yahoo's figure includes the GAAP swings). Uber has been profitable only since 2023, so the 10-year P/E history is short and distorted.

## Risks

- **Autonomous vehicles:** robotaxi operators could build their own networks instead of using Uber's.
- **Competition:** DoorDash and Instacart in delivery, Lyft in US rides, local rivals abroad.
- **Regulation:** driver employment status, and taxes and rules in many countries.
- **Earnings quality:** large revaluation swings on its stakes in GAAP results.
