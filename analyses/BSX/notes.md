# Boston Scientific (BSX): Claude's notes

Updated: 2026-10-27

The evening run copies these sections into the PDF. Update them when the story changes.

## Verdict

**A strong, profitable business whose stock has halved because growth slowed sharply. Cheap against its own history and its peers, but debt is its weak spot. View: wait for the Q3 results on Oct 28, then buy at around $42 (13x adjusted earnings).**

## Summary

- **Cheap:** the lowest valuation in a decade, and cheaper than most medical-device peers.
- **Strong:** the best profitability and cash generation in its peer group.
- **Weak:** little cash against its debt, with more debt coming from the Penumbra deal.
- **Uncertain:** growth has fallen from about 16% to about 5%, and it is not yet clear how much of that is temporary.

## Business

Boston Scientific makes devices for heart, vascular, digestive and urology procedures. The growth engines of the last few years were electrophysiology (pulsed field ablation, Farapulse) and the Watchman left-atrial-appendage closure device. Organic growth was 15.8% in 2025; the 2026 guide is now 5–6% after three cuts. It generates about $3.6bn of free cash flow a year (SEC capex).

## Risks

- **Share loss in US electrophysiology:** competitors' pulsed field ablation systems are taking share.
- **Watchman:** stand-alone Watchman procedures are falling.
- **Debt:** the pending $14.5bn Penumbra deal adds about $11bn of debt, taking net debt to EBITDA near 3.8x; it also needs an FTC remedy.
- **Cyberattack:** the Aug 25 attack disrupted manufacturing and shipping; likely a one-off but it clouds Q3.

## News

- 2026-10-27: Q3 results are due before the open on Oct 28; consensus adjusted EPS is about $0.72.
- 2026: the stock is down about half from its 2025 high after three guidance cuts.

## What to watch

- The Q3 report on Oct 28, above all the 2027 outlook.
- Whether US electrophysiology and Watchman stabilise.
- The Penumbra closing, the FTC remedy and the debt package.
