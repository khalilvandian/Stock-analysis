## Summary

- **Cheap:** about 17x adjusted earnings, against a 10-year norm of about 21x.
- **Strong:** passes every strict debt rule and generates about $9.5bn of free cash flow a year.
- **Weak:** negative shareholders' equity because of buybacks.
- **Uncertain:** Q4 room nights are guided to just +1-3%, and AI agents and regulators (a $900m FTC charge in Q3) are open risks.

## Business

Booking runs Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda, KAYAK and OpenTable. Most of its profit comes from hotel and alternative-accommodation commissions, mainly in Europe. Results are still growing: Q3 2026 adjusted EPS of $4.66 beat the $4.50 consensus and grew 17%. It returns about 10% of its market value to shareholders each year through buybacks and dividends. The stock split 25-for-1 in April 2026.

## Risks

- **AI agents:** assistants that book travel directly (Meta's Muse agent, Sep 23) could cut Booking out of the customer relationship.
- **Slowing volume:** room-night growth fell from 8–9% in 2025 to 5% in Q2 and a guided 3–5% in Q3.
- **Regulators:** an EU Digital Services Act probe (Oct 21), reported FTC action over affiliate hotel ads (Oct 7), and the EU court upholding the ETraveli block (Sep 9).
