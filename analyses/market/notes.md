## Verdict

**The worst week since April 2025: the S&P 500 fell 3.7% on Nov 20 to 6,910, its seventh loss in a row, and is now in a correction, 12% below its high and down 8.6% in a week. The Nasdaq fell 5.3% and is now down for the year. Fear is high (VIX near 36). The trigger was Nvidia's 14% fall on a reported SEC look at its accounting, on top of expected Fed rate hikes. ## Summary

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-21 -->
- **Indices (Nov 20 close):** S&P 500 6,910 (-3.7%), Nasdaq 22,618 (-5.3%), Dow 48,147 (-1.9%); the S&P 500 is 12% below its high and up only 0.8% this year.
- **Cause:** Nvidia fell 14% on a reported SEC inquiry into its accounting; the AI trade is unwinding, and the Fed is expected to raise rates in December.
- **Fear:** the VIX closed near 36, its highest since spring; consumer staples was the only sector up, and Treasuries rallied.
- **Opportunity:** high-quality businesses outside AI are being sold with everything else.

## What happened

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-21 -->
- **Nov 20:** the S&P 500 fell 265 points (3.7%) to 6,910, the Nasdaq 1,261 points (5.3%) to 22,618 and the Dow 950 points (1.9%) to 48,147 ([AP via Yakima Herald](https://www.yakimaherald.com/news/nation_and_world/business/how-major-us-stock-indexes-fared-friday-11-20-2026/article_963ce6d2-85a3-5fd6-981d-e60b72a58fbb.html)). It was the S&P 500's seventh straight loss and its worst week (-8.6%) since April 2025.
- **AI stocks led the fall:** Nvidia -14%, its worst day since the DeepSeek sell-off in January 2025; Broadcom -12% on unconfirmed reports that Google will cut next year's chip orders ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/20/stock-market-today-nov-20-stocks-plunge-as-ai-trade-unwinds/)).
- **Anthropic** gave back most of its debut gain, closing at $88.91 (-8.5%), just above its $83 IPO price ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/20/investing/us-stocks-anthropic-ai)).
- **Safe havens:** consumer staples was the only sector to rise; Treasuries rallied (the 10-year yield fell to about 4.9%); gold fell about 4% and bitcoin about 5%.

## Why it happened

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-21 -->
Three worries are now stacked on top of each other:

1. **Trust in the AI leader.** A report that the SEC is examining Nvidia's accounting ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/20/stock-market-today-nov-20-stocks-plunge-as-ai-trade-unwinds/)) hit the company at the centre of the AI boom, a week after its CEO resigned. When the biggest AI supplier is in doubt, investors sell everything connected to AI spending.
2. **Less AI spending.** The unconfirmed report that Google may cut chip orders suggests the biggest buyers could slow their spending, which is what investors fear most for chipmakers.
3. **Higher rates.** The Fed's minutes (Nov 18) showed almost all officials expect more rate hikes ([CNBC](https://cnbc.com/2026/11/18/fed-minutes-october-2026.html)); the next meeting is Dec 8-9.

The fall in Treasury yields on Friday shows investors moving money to safety, not betting on growth.

## What it means

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-21 -->
In plain terms: the market is now in a real correction, driven by fear about AI and rates rather than by falling profits outside tech. When the VIX is near 36, prices fall further than the news justifies, in both good and bad companies.

For a Lynch-style investor:

- **This is the time the watch list was built for.** Booking, Netflix, Boston Scientific, Uber and Alphabet are all at or close to their entry prices. Their businesses haven't changed this week.
- **Buy in steps, not all at once.** Nobody knows where a correction ends; spreading purchases over several weeks (for example a third now, a third in two weeks, a third after the Fed's Dec 8-9 meeting) avoids guessing the bottom.
- **Avoid the falling knives you can't judge.** Nvidia now screens as cheap (15x), but an accounting inquiry is exactly the kind of uncertainty Lynch says to stay away from until it is resolved.
