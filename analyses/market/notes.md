## Verdict

**The market as a whole is calm and near its high; the stress is in oil, bond yields and a few sold-off groups (software and data companies feared to lose to AI). That is where the bargains are.**

## Summary

- **Indices:** the S&P 500 is about 1% below its high and up about 13% this year.
- **Pressure:** oil is near $100 and the US 10-year yield near 5%, both up sharply this year.
- **Fear:** the VIX is about 18, normal levels.
- **Opportunity:** software and data names sold off on AI fears still have growing earnings and are near decade-low valuations.

## What is driving the market

On Oct 27 the S&P 500 rose about 0.7% to about 7,730 and the Nasdaq about 1.3%, led by chips and Big Tech ahead of their earnings and this week's Fed meeting. Oil topped $100 a barrel in September on Middle East supply worries, and the 10-year yield broke 5% for the first time since 2023 on inflation fears and the chance of Fed rate rises. The index has held up because the largest companies keep growing earnings, but breadth is middling: a little under 60% of S&P 500 stocks are above their 200-day average.

## Stocks to look at

From tonight the list comes from the market screener, not the panic-sell screen. 169 of the 497 S&P 500 companies pass Stage 1 (good financial statements for their Lynch category), and 29 of those are cheap or close to cheap: the top 3 in each of 14 sectors.

- **Cheapest against their own history:** Adobe, Zoetis, Intuit, Incyte and Gartner sit 40-60% below their own cheap price. Adobe and Intuit are the same AI-fear names the October 25 screen found, now confirmed by the statement checks.
- **Check before trusting:** Allstate (5.2x) and Comcast (5.8x) look cheap because recent earnings are unusually high (a light catastrophe year; one-off gains), and Valero's refining margins are at a peak with oil near $100. Cyclicals look cheapest exactly when earnings peak.
- **On the watch list too:** Booking is in the list (fast grower, about 15% below its cheap price on adjusted EPS).

Each name still needs step 1 (absolute values with history) before any decision.

## What to watch

- Oil and the 10-year yield: a move back below 4.5% would ease the pressure on valuations.
- Earnings season this week and next, including BSX (Oct 28) and BKNG (Nov 3) from the watch list.
