## What is driving the market

AI stocks are being sold hard. Doubts about the return on AI spending, first raised by Alphabet's and Intel's results in late October, turned into a broad sell-off: on Nov 5 the S&P 500 had its worst day since the April 2025 tariff shock, and after a short rebound it fell again for three sessions, about 1.9% on Nov 16 and 1% on Nov 17, closing at about 7,324 on Nov 18. Nvidia has lost more than $2 trillion of market value since its October peak. The US midterm elections (Nov 3) are over and no longer a factor. The 10-year yield near 5.1% keeps pressure on valuations, while oil has fallen back to about $83. Breadth is weakening: about 57% of S&P 500 stocks are above their 200-day average.
