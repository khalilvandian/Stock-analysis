## Today

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-19 -->
The stock closed at $283.8 on Nov 18, down about 4% since Nov 13 as AI stocks sold off.

- **Why it fell:** investors are selling companies that spend heavily on AI, and Alphabet spends about $130bn a year on it. A new US rule on AI-chip exports (Nov 16) and turmoil at Nvidia added to the worry.
- **The Anthropic IPO (priced Nov 18, trading from Nov 19):** at $83 a share, Alphabet's roughly 1.79bn Anthropic shares are worth about $149bn, about $12 a share before tax. With SpaceX (about $84bn), that roughly confirms the $15 a share after tax used for the stakes, so the entry price does not change. Anthropic's filing also commits at least $121bn of spending to Google Cloud through 2033, which supports Cloud's growth.

**What it means for you:** the IPO turns a private, hard-to-value stake into a listed one. From now on its price moves will swing Alphabet's reported (GAAP) earnings every quarter; the core P/E used here leaves them out. The risk is concentration: Anthropic is both a big Cloud customer and a rival to Gemini. At 24.6x the stock is fairly priced, not cheap, so the rule is still to wait.

## Summary

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-19 -->
- **Cheap:** not yet; about 24.6x core earnings once the stakes are taken out of the price, a little above its 10-year median of about 24x.
- **Strong:** about $240bn of cash against about $113bn of debt, revenue up about 25% and an operating margin near 32%.
- **Weak:** capital spending of about $130bn a year has turned the quarterly free cash flow margin negative (about -7% in Q3).
- **Uncertain:** whether AI search answers erode Search (still about half of revenue), and how Anthropic's listed price will swing reported EPS.

## Business

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-19 -->
Alphabet owns Google Search and advertising, YouTube, Google Cloud, Android and Waymo. Q3 2026 revenue was about $127.6bn, up about 25% a year, in line with estimates; Search grew about 12%. Reported EPS swings with the marked value of its equity stakes (mainly SpaceX): gains added $2.35 a share in Q1 2026 and $6.26 in Q2, and a loss took $0.55 off Q3. The adjusted EPS here leaves those out (see `eps_override` in the config), so the trailing figure is about $10.95, not Yahoo's $19.16. Following report 17, the after-tax value of the stakes ($14.93 a share at 30 September: Anthropic at its last round, SpaceX, other listed stakes) is also taken out of the price for the P/E and added back to the entry price.

## Risks

<!-- reviewed 2026-11-19 -->
- **Capital spending:** the AI build-out (data centres, chips) is at a record and may not earn its cost.
- **Search disruption:** AI chat assistants compete for the queries that carry Search's advertising.
- **Regulation:** US and EU antitrust cases over Search and advertising technology.
- **Earnings quality:** large unrealized gains and losses on private stakes make GAAP EPS noisy.
