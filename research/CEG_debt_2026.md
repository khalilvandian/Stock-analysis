# CEG: why debt rose in 2026 (researched 2026-09-27)

Total debt went from $9.0B (Dec 2025) to $24.7B (Jun 2026), per `analysis/debt.py`.

## Sources of the increase
- **Calpine acquisition, closed 2026-01-07.** Enterprise value about $26.6B. Purchase price about $22.3B:
  $17.5B in stock (49.4M shares), $2.5B cash net of cash acquired, $2.3B of Calpine notes exchanged
  for CEG notes. CEG assumed about $12.6B of Calpine debt ($7.6B notes and term loans, about $5B project financing).
- **Paydown after close:** repaid $2.5B of Calpine first-lien term loans in Jan and redeemed $1.25B of 2028
  first-lien notes in Feb. H1 2026 totals: $5.0B of long-term debt issued, $5.35B retired.
- **Short-term borrowings** rose from $1.65B to $5.23B. They helped fund the cash part of the deal,
  $2.0B of buybacks and $2.5B of capex while cash fell from $3.6B to $0.7B.

## Where the money is going
- 2026 growth capex of $3.9B: nuclear uprates (about 1 GW), the Crane (Three Mile Island Unit 1) restart
  for Microsoft (835 MW, 2027), the Clinton uprate for Meta, and Wolf Hollow 2 gas peakers (300 MW, TX).
- Buyback authorization raised to $5.0B (Mar 2026); dividend targeted to grow 10% a year.
- Required divestitures (six plants in PA, DE and TX, plus Brazos Valley sold to LS Power for $860M)
  generate proceeds that go toward reducing debt.

## Credit
- Ratings affirmed after the deal: Baa1 (Moody's) and BBB+ (S&P). Moody's lowered the FFO/debt
  level that would trigger a downgrade from 30% to 25%.
