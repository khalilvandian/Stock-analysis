    How the numbers were made

    • The company list is Yahoo Finance's Utilities sector screen for US exchanges. Preferred shares, notes, warrants and duplicate share classes are removed.
    • Last-12-month figures sum the latest four quarters. Where quarterly data is missing, the latest fiscal year is used.
    • DCF fair value (standard): cash flow before growth investment (operating cash flow minus depreciation plus after-tax interest), growing 2.5% a year forever. It's discounted at a WACC built from the 5.18% 10-year Treasury, a 5% equity premium, adjusted beta and a debt spread by leverage, and net debt is subtracted. Companies with negative cash flow show "–".
    • CEG, VST, NRG and TLN use the guidance-based enterprise DCF from the earlier analysis (company free cash flow guidance, 4% growth).
    • EBITDA and earnings are reported (GAAP) figures, so hedge accounting and one-off items can swing them. Ratios on negative EBITDA are shown as "–".
    • This is a screen for comparison, not investment advice.
