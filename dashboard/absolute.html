How to read it

  • The list of values comes from config/absolute_values.toml. Adding a value there adds it to this page on the next build.
  • Latest 12 months sums the last four quarters; balance-sheet values (debt, cash) are the latest quarter-end.
  • History uses fiscal years. Most utilities end their year in December; a few end in March, June or September.
  • EBITDA in the history is operating income plus depreciation from SEC filings, so it can differ from the latest-12-month EBITDA, which comes from Yahoo.
  • Capex is shown as a negative number because it is cash going out. Free cash flow is operating cash flow plus capex.
  • Foreign filers are converted at today's exchange rate for every year, so their history also moves with the currency. Market value history is shown only for US-dollar filers.
