Calpine

The largest US natural-gas power generator and largest geothermal producer (The Geysers, CA). About 28 GW, strongest in Texas and California. Bought for about $22bn in equity, $26.6bn including debt.

Calpine debt at Dec 31, 2025: about $12.5bn

Why CEG bought it: gas plants can run whenever needed, which complements the always-on nuclear fleet. Together they have about 55 GW and 2.5 million retail and business customers.
History: founded 1984 as a geothermal producer at The Geysers. Expanded into gas in the 1990s, went through Chapter 11 in 2005–2008, and was taken private in 2018 by an Energy Capital Partners-led group.
